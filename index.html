During illness and at the end of life

When someone you love is ill, the right words can be hard to find.

You might want to hear their stories, understand their wishes, or simply spend time together without every conversation turning to treatment and test results.

Death Bed Questions offers gentle starting points, whether someone is living with a serious illness, receiving hospice or palliative care, or nearing the end of life. There is room for everyday memories, laughter, uncertainty and the things they most want you to know.

Choose a question together, follow wherever their interest leads, and set aside anything they would rather not answer.
