About Death Bed Questions

Questions that make room for honest conversation

Death Bed Questions began as a personal act of healing. It grew from the questions Jo never got to ask the people she loved before they were gone.

Each card is an invitation to ask deeply, listen wholly and love courageously.

The decks

Seven ways to begin

Six themed decks contain 318 questions. The Mixed Deck Essentials Collection brings selected questions together in one versatile deck.

Roots & Heritage

A gentle exploration of where you come from, what shaped your lineage, and the stories passed down through generations.

Childhood & Becoming

A tender return to the moments and influences that shaped who you became.

Love & Connection

A heart-opening deck designed to deepen closeness, trust, and emotional understanding.

Loss, Regret & Forgiveness

A tender deck for exploring grief, regret, forgiveness, and emotional release.

Meaning & Purpose

A reflective deck for discovering what truly mattered.

Legacy & Letting Go

A sacred deck designed to help you explore endings, legacy, and release.
