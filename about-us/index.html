About Death Bed Questions

The story, the people and the heart behind the questions.

Meet Jo

Jo Cox-Brown is the creator of Death Bed Questions. She’s an Irish writer, seeker, healer and guardian of sacred conversations.

Born from personal loss and love, her work is rooted in a deep belief that honest conversations have the power to heal, connect and transform lives. Through Death Bed Questions, Jo creates spaces where truth can be spoken, stories can be shared, and love can be expressed before it’s too late.

How it began

From one hospital room to six decks.

  1. 01

    Five months to say goodbye

    When Jo’s mum was dying, they had five months together. Every night after work, Jo went to the hospital and they sat with a cup of tea, or a cold Fanta and ice cream on the hot nights, and talked.

  2. 02

    Questions that opened doors

    Some nights it was just one question; other nights they worked through ten or more. Their conversations were full of things they had never explored before, and they helped them both to say goodbye and to heal while they still could.

  3. 03

    Made for the living

    What began as a personal act of healing became six decks, so that no one else has to wonder what someone they love wanted to say. Today they’re used at kitchen tables and bedsides, and by hospice teams, therapists and death doulas.

“The memories are one of my most treasured possessions.”

Jo Cox-Brown

What Jo believes

  • Ask deeply.

    The questions we are most afraid to ask are often the ones that matter most.

  • Listen wholly.

    Being heard, without being fixed or corrected, is a gift in itself.

  • Love courageously.

    Say it now, while there is still time to say it.

The illustrations

Illustrated by Kate Stowe

Kate Stowe is a hospice volunteer who honours the end of life as a sacred threshold. Through her work she has seen the healing power of courageous conversations and open-hearted presence.

Kate lives in Sonoma County, California, with her husband and two teenage sons, and draws her inspiration from the natural world.
