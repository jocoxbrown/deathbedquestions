About Jo Cox-Brown

Jo Cox-Brown is the creator of Death Bed Questions. She's an Irish writer, seeker, healer, and guardian of sacred conversations. Born from personal loss and love, her work is rooted in a deep belief that honest conversations have the power to heal, connect, and transform lives.

“Honest conversations have the power to heal, connect, and transform lives.”

Through Death Bed Questions, Jo creates emotional spaces where truth can be spoken, stories can be shared, and love can be expressed before it’s too late. This deck is part of her wider commitment to fostering meaningful human connection, encouraging us all to ask deeply, listen fully, and live with greater courage and compassion.

Meet Illustrator: Kate Stowe

Kate Stowe, illustrator of Death Bed Questions, is a hospice volunteer who honors the end of life as a sacred threshold. Through her experiences, she has witnessed the healing power of courageous conversations and open-hearted presence in supporting living and dying well.

Kate lives in Sonoma County, California, with her husband and two teenage sons. She spends her days inspired by the natural world, practicing wonder and respect through her art.
