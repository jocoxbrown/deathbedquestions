A gentle guide

How to use the cards

A simple guide to using your cards: how to set the space, what to do during the conversation, and words to open and close your time together.

A session is not about rushing or finding perfect answers. It is about presence, honesty and listening.

Getting started

Five simple steps.

Step I

Set the space

Choose a moment that feels calm, safe and unhurried.

Find somewhere quiet and put your phones away. A candle or some music can help, but all you really need is a little time and each other.

Step II

Choose a card

Use the deck alone, together or in a group. Choose intuitively, take turns or move through the cards in order. There’s no right way.

You might:

  • Choose one card intuitively
  • Select a deck that matches the feeling of the moment
  • Take turns choosing cards for each other
  • Allow the person answering to choose
  • Shuffle the cards and see what comes up

Step III

Make it your own

Welcome reflection, emotion and silence. Listen without interrupting, judging or trying to fix. Passing is always okay.

If a question doesn’t quite fit your life, simply adapt the words to reflect your relationships and experiences.

  • There is no judgement
  • There is no rush
  • Silence is welcome
  • Passing is allowed

Step IV

Respond with kindness

After an answer has been shared, you may respond with kindness.

  • “Thank you for sharing that.”
  • “I’ve never heard that before.”
  • “That helps me understand you more.”

If it feels right, you may ask one gentle follow-up question, but only if the space feels safe.

Step V

Close with care

Take a breath together, sit quietly or write down what you’d like to remember.

End with something soft and grounding: a hug, a shared breath, a final thank you, or a moment of silence.

Who you’re with

One to one, in a group, or on your own.

The steps above work however you gather. Here’s how each way might feel.

  1. One to one

    The most traditional way.

    Sit together somewhere quiet. Take turns choosing a card, and while one of you answers, the other simply listens. Swap if and when it feels right.

  2. In a group

    Around a table with family or friends.

    Pass the deck around so each person draws a card and answers in turn. Others can add a memory when they’re invited to, and the same ground rules apply: no judgement, no rush, and passing is allowed.

  3. On your own

    A quiet conversation with yourself.

    Draw a card and sit with it. Consider journalling what came up and how it made you feel. You might return to the same question another day and notice how your answer has changed.

If emotions rise, let them. If silence comes, honour it.

It’s ok to pause, to breathe, to step away, to allow the tears, and to return or take a new question. This is supposed to be a sacred, kind and gentle space.

Rituals

Words to open and close.

    Before you begin, create a moment of stillness. You may wish to light a candle, hold hands, or sit in silence for a few breaths. Then softly say:

    “We come into this space with open hearts and gentle minds. We honour what has been lived, what has been loved, and what has shaped us. In this moment, there is no past and no future, only truth, only presence, only now. May our words be kind, our listening be deep, and love guide what is shared.”

    Pause. Breathe together. Begin when it feels right.

    When the session feels complete, you may say:

    You may finish with a hug, a hand over the heart, silence, or your own words of thanks.

    To the love that held us.
    To the memories that live on.
    To truth, to healing and to every moment we have left together.
    May we speak with honesty and love without regret.”

    Then drink, breathe, and remain together in quiet presence.
