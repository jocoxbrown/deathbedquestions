A gentle guide

How to use the cards

A session with Death Bed Questions is not about rushing or finding perfect answers. It is about presence, honesty and listening.

Turn each card over to read it.

Getting started

Five gentle steps.

    {% capture back %}

    Allow time & set the space

    Light a candle, dim the lights, put on some soulful music if it feels right. Create a space that feels calm and safe, then take a moment to breathe together.

    You may say: “Today, we open ourselves to truth, to memory, and to whatever needs to be spoken or heard.”

    {% endcapture %} {% include question-card.html shift="0deg" tint="#9A5F3C" label="Step 01" title="Allow time & set the space" back=back %} {% capture back %}

    Agree the ground rules

    • There is no judgement
    • There is no rush
    • Silence is welcome
    • Passing is allowed
    {% endcapture %} {% include question-card.html shift="-30deg" tint="#A85F65" label="Step 02" title="Agree the ground rules" back=back %} {% capture back %}

    Choose your approach

    • Choose one card intuitively
    • Select a deck that matches the feeling of the moment
    • Take turns choosing cards for each other
    • Move sequentially through a deck
    • Allow the person answering to choose
    • Shuffle cards and see what pops out

    There is no correct way. Follow what feels right in the moment.

    {% endcapture %} {% include question-card.html shift="180deg" tint="#4F6475" label="Step 03" title="Choose your approach" back=back %} {% capture back %}

    Ask and listen

    Read the question gently, out loud. Give the person time to sit with it, without interrupting.

    {% endcapture %} {% include question-card.html shift="110deg" tint="#8FA897" label="Step 04" title="Ask and listen" back=back %} {% capture back %}

    Close gently

    End the session with something soft and grounding:

    • A hug
    • A shared breath
    • A final thank you
    • A moment of silence
    {% endcapture %} {% include question-card.html shift="230deg" tint="#7D788C" label="Step 05" title="Close gently" back=back %}

If emotions rise, let them. If silence comes, honour it.

It’s ok to pause, to breathe, to step away, to allow the tears, and to return or take a new question. This is supposed to be a sacred, kind and gentle space.

During the conversation

After an answer has been shared, you may respond with kindness.

  • “Thank you for sharing that.”

  • “I’ve never heard that before.”

  • “That helps me understand you more.”

If it feels right, you may ask one gentle follow-up question, but only if the space feels safe.

You are not here to fix or correct. You are here to witness.

Rituals

Words to open and close.

    {% capture back %}

    Before you begin, create a moment of stillness. You may wish to light a candle, hold hands, or sit in silence for a few breaths. Then softly say:

    “We come into this space with open hearts and gentle minds. We honour what has been lived, what has been loved, and what has shaped us. In this moment, there is no past and no future, only truth, only presence, only now. May our words be kind, our listening be deep, and love guide what is shared.”

    Pause. Breathe together. Begin when it feels right.

    {% endcapture %} {% include question-card.html id="ritual-opening" shift="-15deg" tint="#C79A8F" label="Ritual" title="Opening script" back=back %} {% capture back %}

    When the session feels complete, you may say:

    “We honour what has been spoken and what has been felt. We thank each other for courage, for honesty and for listening. May what has been shared settle gently in our hearts, and may love continue to move through us.”

    You may finish with a hug, a hand over the heart, silence, or your own words of thanks.

    {% endcapture %} {% include question-card.html id="ritual-closing" shift="110deg" tint="#8FA897" label="Ritual" title="Closing script" back=back %} {% capture back %}

    Lift a cup, a glass of water, tea, wine or simply your hands and say:

    “To the stories that shaped us.
    To the love that held us.
    To the memories that live on.
    To truth, to healing and to every moment we have left together.
    May we speak with honesty and love without regret.”

    Then drink, breathe, and remain together in quiet presence.

    {% endcapture %} {% include question-card.html id="toast-blessing" shift="180deg" tint="#4F6475" label="Ritual" title="Toast & blessing" back=back %}

On your own

If using the cards alone, consider journalling what came up and how it made you feel.

These conversations are sacred. Let them settle in your heart.
